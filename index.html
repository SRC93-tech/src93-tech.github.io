Hi there 👋

I make StockGrid, an Android app that garment manufacturers, wholesalers and retailers use to run their stock. It keeps a firm's catalog, its stock in every size and colour, and a complete history of every stock movement on the owner's and the staff's phones. It's on Google Play, and garment businesses use it every day.

StockGrid

It's a Flutter app on a Supabase backend (PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions). A few things I care about in it:

  • Each firm's data is kept apart by PostgreSQL row-level security, not just by the app.
  • Stock is a ledger. Mistakes are corrected with a new entry; history is never erased.
  • Heavy reports run inside the database, so a phone downloads a few hundred rows instead of a year of history.
  • Backups are encrypted on the phone before they go to Google Drive.
  • Every change goes through automated tests before it ships, and database changes are tried on a separate test project first.
